## Our Mission

KATDL is a technology and software engineering firm that builds tools, data systems, and research infrastructure — with deep roots in health sciences and growing work in areas like climate data and budget modeling.

We partner with scientists, researchers, clinicians, and organizations to solve complex problems through custom software, data analysis, and applied AI.

We have backgrounds in science and academics, combined with deep experience building and securing large-scale software systems. We know how to communicate about technical work clearly — and how to deliver.

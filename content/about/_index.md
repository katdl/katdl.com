## Our Mission

KATDL is a technology and software engineering firm that builds tools, data systems, and research infrastructure for health sciences.

We partner with doctors, scientists, researchers, and health organizations to solve complex problems through custom software, data analysis, and applied AI.

We have backgrounds in science and academics, combined with deep experience building and securing large-scale software systems. We know how to communicate about technical work clearly — and how to deliver.
